# Inline formatting, links, images, and footnotes

[Documentation](../README.md) · [中文](inline-and-media.zh.md)

In Live Preview, ParaFlow reveals inline formatting source during editing and provides editing and navigation for links, images, and footnotes.

## Use

- **Inline formatting:** enter an editing position in bold, italic, inline code, or other formatted content to reveal its source; move away to restore rendering. Subscript and superscript examples include `H~2~O` and `x^2^`.
- **Links:** by default, a plain click enters link source editing, and `Ctrl`-click opens the link. Settings can change a plain click to open the link.
- **Images:** with “Reveal source at editing positions” enabled, clicking an image or moving the caret to its edge reveals an editable image link. Standalone images can be centered; explicit size and alignment settings contribute to their display.
- **Footnotes:** follow a reference to its definition and return to the body. The footnote name and definition remain available during editing, with the complete Markdown markers available for inspection.

## Settings

Under **Settings → ParaFlow → Editing**, adjust these groups independently:

| Group | Main settings |
| --- | --- |
| Inline formatting & links | Plain-click behavior, subscript, superscript, and Obsidian comment display |
| Images | Reveal source at editing positions and center standalone images |
| Footnotes | Enhanced footnote display and navigation |

Obsidian comments can remain visible or appear only while editing. Switch to Source view to inspect link destinations, image paths, and footnote markers.
